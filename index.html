The Far Side

Every highlight has a story outside the frame.

Social media shows us outcomes: the acceptance, the celebration, the smiling group photo. It rarely shows the rejection, the homesickness, the pressure standing just outside the frame.

Both sides can be true at once. Someone can be proud of an achievement and still feel lonely, exhausted, or uncertain.

The stories on the far side are clearly labelled samples, written for this demo. Anything you write stays in your own browser. Support resources →

A note on care

This is an art piece, not a crisis service

The Far Side cannot reply to you, and nobody is monitoring what you write here. If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, please talk to a real person tonight.

  • US & Canada: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • UK & Ireland: call 116 123 (Samaritans)
  • Anywhere: findahelpline.com
  • On a campus: your counselling centre almost certainly has same-week urgent slots.

The Moon could not launch

This browser has no WebGL

The Far Side draws a real Moon, so it needs hardware-accelerated graphics. Try Chrome, Edge, Safari 16+ or Firefox with hardware acceleration switched on — or open it on a phone.

The idea survives without the Moon, though: the post you saw is not the whole story, and asking someone how they actually are costs nothing.
