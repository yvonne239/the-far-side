A note on care

This is an art piece, not a crisis service

Moonflip cannot reply to you, and nobody is monitoring what you write here. If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, please talk to a real person tonight.

  • US & Canada: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • UK & Ireland: call 116 123 (Samaritans)
  • Anywhere: findahelpline.com
  • On a campus: your counselling centre almost certainly has same-week urgent slots.

The Moon could not launch

This browser has no WebGL

Moonflip draws a real Moon, so it needs hardware-accelerated graphics. Try Chrome, Edge, Safari 16+ or Firefox with hardware acceleration switched on — or open it on a phone.

The idea survives without the Moon, though. Whatever you are looking at — a person's good news, a QR code, a photo disappearing into "the cloud" — there is a whole other half of it pointed away from you.
